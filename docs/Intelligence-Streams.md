# Regional Intelligence Streams
Valley Hub aggregates over 120 verified news sources across the Lehigh Valley, from major broadcasters to hyperlocal blogs.
## Information Quality Score (IQS)
Each feed is assigned an IQS (0-100) based on three primary metrics:
1. **Frequency (40pts)**: How often the source publishes. High-velocity feeds (>3/week) score higher.
2. **Latency (30pts)**: The delay between a real-world event and the RSS update. Updates within 6 hours are optimal.
3. **Density (30pts)**: Semantic richness. Sources providing full summaries or descriptive content score higher than "headline-only" feeds.
## Mesh Consensus
While IQS is calculated programmatically by the Durable Object, nodes can "vote" (Thumbs Up/Down) on feed quality. The **Consensus Score** displayed in the UI represents a weighted average of global node feedback, providing a decentralized filter against misinformation or broken feeds.
